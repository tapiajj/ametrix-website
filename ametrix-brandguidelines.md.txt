# PART 1 — BRAND GUIDELINES

## 2. Logo Identity

### Primary Logo

The Ametrix Technologies logo consists of:

1. A geometric gem/database symbol.
2. The lowercase wordmark `ametrix`.
3. The secondary descriptor `technologies`.

### Symbol Construction

The symbol must preserve:

- A six-sided geometric top surface.
- Three stacked database-inspired sections.
- Symmetrical left and right faces.
- Consistent line thickness.
- Clean geometric intersections.
- Flat colors without gradients.
- Recognizable structure at smaller sizes.

The logo should never be redesigned as a generic
cube, traditional database cylinder, or gemstone
without its database structure.

### Approved Logo Variations

**Primary Horizontal**

Symbol on the left, wordmark on the right.

**Stacked**

Symbol centered above the wordmark.

**Symbol Only**

Gem/database icon without accompanying text.

**Monochrome**

Entire logo rendered in one solid color.

**Reversed**

Light-colored logo on a dark background.

### Logo Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo
equivalent to the height of the lowercase "a"
in the wordmark.

No text, decorative graphics, or other logos should
enter this area.

### Minimum Size Recommendations

| Application | Minimum Width |
|---|---|
| Full logo, digital | 180 px |
| Icon only, digital | 24 px |
| Full logo, print | 40 mm |
| Icon only, print | 8 mm |

These are starting recommendations. Verify
legibility using the final vector artwork.

### Incorrect Logo Usage

Do not:

- Stretch or distort the logo.
- Rotate the symbol independently.
- Alter the number of database layers.
- Change the six-sided top geometry.
- Add shadows, bevels, or glow effects.
- Apply gradients to the primary logo.
- Use insufficient contrast.
- Change the wordmark proportions.
- Recreate the icon with inconsistent strokes.

## 3. Color System

Ametrix uses two complementary visual themes.

The light theme emphasizes clarity, precision,
and technical professionalism.

The dark theme emphasizes confidence, energy,
and a subtle retro-technology influence.

Both themes share a recognizable blue/cyan identity.

### 3.1 Light Theme — Modern Blue

| Role | Color | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Soft White | #F8FAFC |
| Primary Text | Deep Navy | #0F2D5B |
| Primary Accent | Electric Blue | #2497E8 |
| Secondary Accent | Medium Blue | #459DCE |

**Recommended Logo Combination**

- Background: `#F8FAFC`
- Gem/database icon: `#0F2D5B`
- "ametrix": `#0F2D5B`
- "technologies": `#2497E8`

**Applications**

- Corporate documents
- Proposals and reports
- Website light mode
- Business cards
- Presentation slides
- Printed marketing materials

### 3.2 Dark Theme — Midnight & Retro Orange

| Role | Color | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Midnight Charcoal | #0E1117 |
| Primary Accent | Electric Cyan | #22B8E6 |
| Primary Text | Warm Cream | #FFEEDB |
| Secondary Accent | Retro Orange | #FF8A52 |

**Recommended Logo Combination**

- Background: `#0E1117`
- Gem/database icon: `#22B8E6`
- "ametrix": `#FFEEDB`
- "technologies": `#FF8A52`

**Applications**

- Website dark mode
- Developer-focused materials
- Technology presentations
- Social media
- Digital advertising
- Event graphics

### 3.3 Color Usage Rules

- Use navy or charcoal as the primary structural color.
- Use blue/cyan to establish brand recognition.
- Reserve orange for selective emphasis.
- Avoid introducing additional saturated colors.
- Keep typography readable against its background.
- Use solid color fills for the primary logo.

### 3.4 Accessibility

Aim for WCAG AA contrast:

- 4.5:1 for standard body text.
- 3:1 for large text and important UI graphics.

Some brand accents may not meet these requirements
on every background.

Use deeper colors for small text on light backgrounds
and lighter colors for small text on dark backgrounds.

## 4. Typography

### Recommended Primary Typeface

**Inter**

Use for:

- Website headings
- Product interfaces
- Marketing materials
- Company presentations
- Business documents

Suggested weights:

| Element | Weight |
|---|---|
| Main heading | 700 |
| Section heading | 600 |
| Subheading | 500 |
| Body text | 400 |
| Caption | 400 |

### Alternative Typefaces

- Manrope — modern and geometric
- Plus Jakarta Sans — friendly and contemporary
- IBM Plex Sans — technical and professional

### Wordmark Guidance

The existing `ametrix` wordmark should retain
its bold lowercase appearance.

The `technologies` descriptor should remain
lighter and visually secondary.

Do not replace the established wordmark typography
without evaluating the entire logo system.

## 5. Visual Design Language

### Geometry

Favor:

- Straight lines
- Geometric forms
- Symmetrical compositions
- Consistent spacing
- Clean iconography
- Subtle corner rounding in interfaces

### Backgrounds

Approved:

- Solid white
- Soft white
- Deep navy
- Midnight charcoal
- Solid brand accent colors

Decorative technical grids may be used in
supporting graphics, but not inside the master logo.

### Effects

The primary brand identity should remain flat.

Avoid:

- Gradients
- Drop shadows
- Metallic effects
- 3D rendering
- Neon glow
- Excessive textures

A retro aesthetic may be introduced through
typography, composition, geometric patterns,
and orange accents rather than visual effects.

---

## 6. Print Guidelines

### Master Artwork

Maintain a vector master in SVG, PDF, or EPS format.

All icon geometry should be constructed using
clean vector paths.

### Printing Requirements

- Use flat solid colors.
- Convert colors to the printer's required profile.
- Verify CMYK conversions with a printed proof.
- Keep a monochrome logo available.
- Avoid very thin lines at small sizes.
- Outline fonts when required by the print vendor.
- Maintain sufficient clear space.

### Embroidery and Merchandise

Use a simplified one-color version when necessary.

Check that:

- Internal openings remain visible.
- Lines do not merge.
- Small text remains readable.
- Thread or ink colors maintain contrast.

### Recommended Deliverables

- Primary logo — SVG
- Light theme logo — SVG
- Dark theme logo — SVG
- Black monochrome logo — SVG
- White monochrome logo — SVG
- Transparent PNG exports
- Print-ready vector PDF

**Important:** Raster logo previews are not
substitutes for final production vector artwork.
